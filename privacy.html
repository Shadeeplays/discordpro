TylerAI Role System Privacy Policy

TylerAI operates in role-only mode for one Discord community. It maintains basic and tenure roles and lets members choose five alert or interest roles. It does not scan messages, reactions, attachments, or voice activity, and it does not use AI to profile members.

What the bot processes

  • Discord server, user, role, channel, and panel-message identifiers needed to maintain roles.
  • Whether an account is a bot, whether membership screening is complete, the current server join timestamp, and the member’s current role IDs.
  • Button and select-menu submissions used immediately to replace only the five member-selectable roles.
  • Content-free operational audits recording a role action, outcome, reason, and time.

What the bot does not keep

TylerAI does not subscribe to message, reaction, attachment, or voice events in role-only mode. It does not inspect or store message text, attachment files or URLs, voice audio, AI prompts, direct messages, behavioral metrics, or a separate profile of a member’s panel choices.

Role decisions

TylerAI gives the existing Member role to screened human members and a permissionless Veteran role when the current Discord join timestamp reaches its first calendar anniversary. Rejoining resets the join timestamp available to the bot. Members use /awards roles or the private selector in 🤖choose-roles to choose Stream Alerts, Event Alerts, Community Updates, Looking to Play, and Creator Collabs. Submitting the selector replaces only those five roles.

Activity features are sealed

The eight habit awards, activity-based VIP selection, historical scanning, and all new behavioral collection are dormant. They cannot be activated by the ordinary pause command. Activation requires written Discord authorization for the exact use, updated published policies, a versioned server notice, and another seven-day opt-out window.

Retention and security

  • Role configuration, registered Discord role IDs, panel state, resumable operation cursors, and content-free role audits are stored on the operator’s Windows host.
  • Before the approved legacy-role reset, TylerAI stores an AES-256-GCM encrypted restoration snapshot containing the affected role definitions, assignments, hierarchy, and channel overwrites. It is derived from the private installation secret and deleted after seven days.
  • Daily SQLite backups rotate after seven days.
  • Logs exclude messages, attachments, credentials, AI prompts, and behavioral profiles.

Your choices

  • /awards roles shows current opt-ins and opens the private selector. Selecting nothing clears all five optional roles.
  • /awards privacy action:status reports whether any legacy activity data remains.
  • /awards privacy action:delete deletes legacy metrics, evidence, preferences, and awards and anonymizes historical audit entries. Rotating backups age out within seven days.

Sharing and service providers

TylerAI does not sell member data or use it for advertising. Data is exchanged with Discord only as needed to maintain server roles and is stored on the community operator’s Windows host. No AI provider receives member data. Discord processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

Questions or changes

Contact the server administrators through the Discord server for privacy questions or requests that cannot be completed with the commands above. Material policy changes will be announced before they take effect. Behavioral processing remains sealed unless every activation condition above is met.
