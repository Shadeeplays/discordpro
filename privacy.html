TylerAI Habit Awards Privacy Policy

TylerAI recognizes positive, measurable participation in one Discord community. It does not use an AI model to judge personalities, relationships, or message meaning.

What the bot processes

  • Discord account, server, channel, role, message, reply, reaction, and event identifiers needed to calculate and maintain awards.
  • Message timestamps, the number of non-whitespace characters, whether a message appears to be a command or recent duplicate, and whether it has attachments.
  • Reply relationships and aggregate non-self reaction counts. Raw reaction-user identities are used transiently to deduplicate and exclude bots, nonmembers, and self-reactions, then discarded; a private keyed hash may be retained temporarily as deduplication evidence.
  • Voice-state timestamps used to count eligible minutes when at least two human members are present. TylerAI never records or processes voice audio.
  • A member’s award status, progress totals, privacy choice, and optional notification preference.

What the bot does not keep

Message text is inspected transiently for the limited checks above and is never written to TylerAI’s database, backups, or logs. Attachments are never downloaded or inspected, and attachment URLs are not stored. The service does not store AI prompts, direct messages, or activity from private, NSFW, archive, log, or restricted channels. Staff never receive community award roles; their qualifying messages and support replies also produce private staff summaries, while human replies or reactions may contribute to another member’s structural award evidence.

Purpose and award decisions

TylerAI uses published thresholds to assign eight permissionless Discord roles. Members may view the exact rules with /awards catalog and their own progress with /awards me. Server staff are excluded from community awards. There is no public leaderboard or public award announcement.

Historical scanning

Historical message scanning is disabled by default. It may run only after the server posts a privacy notice, waits at least seven days, and obtains any approval required by Discord. If those conditions are not met, calculations begin with new activity. Historical voice activity is never scanned. At finalization, older per-member bootstrap data outside the ordinary 45-day live-retention limit is deleted or converted to anonymous server totals; recent activity may remain only within that same 45-day limit. If a safety circuit breaker blocks an award batch, TylerAI may retain only its month-level qualification result under a private keyed recovery hash—not a raw Discord account identifier—for no more than 30 days. That limited proof exists solely so staff can retry the blocked batch and is deleted sooner after recovery or a member’s privacy-deletion request.

Retention and security

  • A content-free message index is retained for no more than 48 hours.
  • For ordinary live collection, per-member metrics and hashed deduplication or reaction evidence are retained for no more than 45 days. During an approved historical scan, older bootstrap aggregates exist only until assignment and scan finalization, then are deleted or converted to anonymous server totals; canceled scans are rolled back. The narrowly limited keyed recovery proof described above expires within 30 days and is removed from retained backups before their own expiration could extend that limit.
  • Daily SQLite backups are sanitized so they do not extend the live-data limits above, stored on the operator’s Windows host, and removed no later than seven days after creation.
  • Older reporting is anonymous and server-level. Audit entries are anonymized when a member deletes their data.
  • Logs exclude message content, attachment data, credentials, and raw user identifiers.

Your choices

  • /awards privacy action:status explains your current participation state.
  • /awards privacy action:opt-out stops collection and removes TylerAI award roles.
  • /awards privacy action:delete deletes your active metrics, evidence, preferences, and awards and anonymizes audit history. Rotating backups age out within seven days.
  • /awards notifications enabled:true explicitly opts in to private award messages. They are off by default.

Sharing and service providers

TylerAI does not sell member data or use it for advertising. Data is exchanged with Discord only as needed to operate the bot and is stored on the community operator’s Windows host. No AI provider receives activity data. Discord processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

Questions or changes

Contact the server administrators through the Discord server for privacy questions or requests that cannot be completed with the commands above. Material policy changes will be announced in the server before they take effect. Collection can be paused by an administrator at any time.
