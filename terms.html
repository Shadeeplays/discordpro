What TylerAI provides

TylerAI applies published, activity-based thresholds and may assign up to eight decorative Discord roles. Awards have no permissions, monetary value, ownership right, rank, or guarantee of access. They are community recognition only.

Fair participation

Do not spam, automate activity, coordinate fake replies or reactions, idle in voice channels to farm time, or otherwise manipulate award measurements. Commands, duplicate content, self-reactions, bot activity, and other ineligible events do not count. Server rules continue to apply.

Award changes and removals

Qualifications are normally evaluated weekly. A role may be renewed, enter a grace period, or be removed when activity no longer qualifies. TylerAI may pause a batch when a safety check detects unusual volume, missing permissions, an invalid role hierarchy, or a badge-integrity problem. Administrators may pause the service to investigate errors or abuse.

No staff ranking

The server owner and members with Administrator or Manage Server permission do not receive community award roles. Private operational staff summaries are not public rankings.

The Privacy Policy explains the limited activity data used by the bot. You may inspect your status, opt out, or request deletion through /awards privacy. Award notifications are private and disabled unless you explicitly enable them.
