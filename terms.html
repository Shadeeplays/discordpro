TylerAI Role System Terms of Use

These terms govern the TylerAI role-only bot in its configured Discord community. Using its commands or selector means you agree to these terms and Discord’s applicable terms.

What TylerAI provides

TylerAI maintains the existing Member role, a permissionless Veteran role based on the current server join timestamp, and five permissionless alert or interest roles chosen by members. These roles have no monetary value, ownership right, rank, or guarantee of access.

Your role choices

The selector changes only Stream Alerts, Event Alerts, Community Updates, Looking to Play, and Creator Collabs. Veteran, VIP, awards, Member, staff, and integration roles cannot be selected. Server rules continue to apply.

Role changes and recovery

TylerAI may reconcile missing Member or Veteran assignments and repair its registered permissionless roles. Unknown roles are reported but never automatically deleted. The approved legacy-role cleanup requires a fingerprinted preview and an exact confirmation token; encrypted restoration data expires after seven days.

Dormant awards and VIP

TylerAI does not currently score messages, replies, reactions, media, promotions, or voice activity. Habit awards and activity-based VIP remain dormant unless Discord authorizes the exact use and the community receives updated policies and notice. Existing award roles may remain decorative, but role-only mode does not newly earn or remove them.

Privacy and opting out

The Privacy Policy explains the limited role data used by the bot. Optional roles can be cleared with /awards roles, and legacy activity data can be inspected or deleted with /awards privacy.

Availability and disclaimers

TylerAI is provided as available and may be interrupted by Discord, internet, computer, permission, or software failures. Role changes may be delayed or corrected when data is incomplete. Changes and termination

The community operator may change or discontinue TylerAI or these terms to keep the system lawful, secure, and useful. Material changes will be announced in the server. Ending the service does not turn optional or decorative roles into permanent entitlements.
