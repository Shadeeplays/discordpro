TylerAI Community Analytics & Role System Terms of Use

These terms govern TylerAI’s role features and announced community-analytics sweep in its configured Discord community. Using its commands or selector remains subject to these terms and Discord’s applicable terms.

What TylerAI provides

TylerAI maintains the existing Member role, a permissionless Veteran role based on the current server join timestamp, and five permissionless alert or interest roles chosen by members. These roles have no monetary value, ownership right, rank, or guarantee of access.

Your role choices

The selector changes only Stream Alerts, Event Alerts, Community Updates, Looking to Play, and Creator Collabs. Veteran, VIP, awards, Member, staff, and integration roles cannot be selected. Server rules continue to apply.

Role changes and recovery

TylerAI may reconcile missing Member or Veteran assignments and repair its registered permissionless roles. Unknown roles are reported but never automatically deleted. The approved legacy-role cleanup requires a fingerprinted preview and an exact confirmation token; encrypted restoration data expires after seven days.

Announced analytics sweep

TylerAI may perform the historical community-analytics sweep described in the Privacy Policy only after the exact written Discord authorization for that use is recorded, this policy is published, and a versioned server notice completes a full seven-day opt-out window. The sweep may analyze readable server messages, participation metadata, membership tenure, replies, attachment types, and reactions. It excludes direct messages, private threads, NSFW areas, inaccessible channels, attachment contents, and voice audio.

Collection itself does not create or assign activity-based roles. Existing award roles may remain decorative. Any future role criteria remain subject to administrator review and a separate announcement.

Analytics limitations

Analytics and summaries may be incomplete or inaccurate because permissions, inaccessible channels, deleted messages, departed members, and opt-outs affect coverage. Missing coverage is treated as unknown rather than inactivity. Results are advisory and do not guarantee a role, benefit, rank, access right, or permanent status.

Privacy and opting out

The Privacy Policy explains the data scope, local analysis, retention, and exclusions. Optional roles can be cleared with /awards roles. Members can opt out, inspect bounded status, or request deletion with /awards privacy.

Availability and disclaimers

TylerAI is provided as available and may be interrupted by Discord, internet, computer, permission, or software failures. Role changes may be delayed or corrected when data is incomplete. To the extent allowed by law, the community operator disclaims implied warranties and is not responsible for indirect or consequential loss arising from use or unavailability of this free service.

Changes and termination

The community operator may change or discontinue TylerAI or these terms to keep the system lawful, secure, and useful. Material changes will be announced in the server. Ending the service does not turn optional or decorative roles into permanent entitlements.
